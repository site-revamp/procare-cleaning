# ProCare Cleaning Services Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

ProCare Cleaning Services is a Dubai-based professional cleaning company providing residential, commercial and hospitality cleaning solutions. Its target customers include homeowners, hotels, restaurants, offices, healthcare facilities, shopping malls and commercial property managers.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting services more clearly, strengthening business credibility and making it easier for potential customers to submit enquiries. The design follows a professional direction aligned with the brand tagline, "Where Clean Meets Care."

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Service Presentation
- Restructured cleaning services into distinct residential, commercial and hospitality categories.
- Improved the organization of individual services to help visitors identify relevant solutions quickly.
- Made the service offering easier to understand for different customer groups.

### 2. Improved Trust and Credibility
- Addressed the use of zero values in homepage business statistics.
- Improved the presentation of business information to avoid negative first impressions.
- Focused on communicating credibility without relying on unsupported experience figures or fabricated statistics.

### 3. More Direct Enquiry Experience
- Improved the visibility of telephone and email contact options.
- Strengthened calls to action throughout the homepage.
- Made the enquiry journey more straightforward, reducing dependence on the contact form as the primary communication method.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Created a more professional and organized appearance consistent with ProCare's cleaning service identity.
- **Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and separated service categories into clearer sections.
- **User Experience:** Made important services and contact options easier to locate.
- **Consistency:** Established a more structured presentation of service information, business details and enquiry actions.
- **Overall Appearance:** Refined the homepage to communicate ProCare's residential, commercial and hospitality capabilities more effectively.

## 4. Project Summary

The ProCare Cleaning Services homepage redesign addresses three key issues: unclear service organization, weak credibility signals and an ineffective enquiry experience. The updated structure makes the company's service offering easier to understand while providing visitors with clearer ways to contact the business.
